# ADR-027: Domain simplification (Workspace consolidation, inline Targets, unified Schedule)

![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-Accepted-brightgreen) ![Date](https://img.shields.io/badge/date-2026--06--10-blue)

## Context

boop's authoring surface accreted three independent structural choices that compound into felt complexity: a three-tier scope lattice (workspace → customer → job) on Channels and AlertRules per [ADR-025](025-workspace-scope-on-channels-and-alert-rules.md), a separate admin-only `Target` entity that a Job must reference but which lives tucked inside the Customer hub per [ADR-018](018-navigation-ia.md), and a three-way `Trigger` (cron, interval, webhook) where cron and interval are the same idea (a time-based cadence) split across two nullable column sets and two dispatch lanes. The on-ramp is "create a Customer, then a Target, then a Job, then pick cron or interval," and the scope question "where does this Channel live?" has three answers. The cost of leaving this is a product that reads as disjointed for what is, in practice, a single team scheduling HTTP calls.

The unstated assumption underneath all three is the MSP framing: SwitchThink as one tenant managing ~270 Customer organizations. ADR-025 in particular exists to make that 270-Customer fan-out ergonomic. If the Customer tier is not load-bearing in daily use, every structure built to manage 270 of them is ceremony, and the simplification is real rather than cosmetic.

## Decision

Collapse the org model to a single tier called **Workspace** (replacing Customer), inline Target authoring into the Job, and present the time-based Trigger as one **Schedule**. The runtime dispatch core ([ADR-014](014-two-lane-dispatch.md)) is unchanged; this is a tenancy, authoring, and IA decision, not a dispatch rewrite.

| Concern | Choice | Over |
|---|---|---|
| Org tier | **One tier: `Workspace`, replacing `Customer`, and in v1, exactly one Workspace.** A default Workspace is seeded and every surface resolves to it. Multi-Workspace support (additional Workspaces for per-client isolation) is deferred until the tier earns its keep by actually scoping visibility, permissions, or routing; the schema keeps `workspace_id` everywhere so it returns additively. | Keeping Customer under a Workspace tenant (the two-level model ADR-018 / ADR-025 assume); a pure rename of Customer to Workspace that keeps the global tier (renames the pain, reduces nothing); shipping a multi-Workspace switcher before the tier scopes anything (ceremony over one Workspace). |
| Global scope folds in | **The former `scope='workspace'` (global, cross-Customer) Channels and AlertRules fold into the default Workspace.** There is no cross-Workspace shared tier in v1. | Preserving a global tier above Workspace (would make this a rename, not a reduction; scope count stays at three). |
| Channel scope | **A `Channel` belongs to exactly one `Workspace`.** The `scope` discriminator, the nullable `customer_id`, and the CHECK constraint from ADR-025 are removed. | The workspace/customer dual scope (the lattice this ADR exists to remove). |
| AlertRule scope | **`workspace` or `job`, additive.** A Run is evaluated against its Workspace's rules and its Job's rules; the union of matching rules' Channels is the deduped fan-out set. The `customer` tier is removed. | The three-tier workspace/customer/job lattice (ADR-025); shadowing/precedence semantics (additive stays). |
| Target ↔ Job | **Target stays a reusable entity owned by the Workspace, but is pick-or-create inline from the Job form and renders inline on the Job.** Operators (not only Admins) may create Targets. | Folding url/method/auth into the Job row (loses cross-Job reuse and single-point auth rotation); keeping the admin-gated, separately-navigated Target (the "make a Target first" detour). |
| Trigger presentation | **Two user-facing kinds: `Schedule` (time-based) and `Webhook`.** A Schedule is expressed as a preset, interval, advanced cron, or natural language; boop resolves it to a stored cron expression or `interval_seconds` and selects the dispatch lane. The operator never picks cron-vs-interval-vs-lane. | Exposing cron and interval as distinct user choices (the split the user experiences as needless). |
| NL Schedule authoring | **A natural-language box ("every weekday at 9am ET") proposes a Schedule**, riding the Draft / Tool-catalog machinery of [ADR-015](015-ai-authoring-stack.md) (a `propose_schedule`-shaped tool), validated and previewed (next fire times) via [ADR-017](017-cron-and-time.md)'s `croner` before confirm. | A free-text cron field as the only "advanced" path; deferring any NL affordance (the user asked for it in this pass). |
| Navigation / URL | **Jobs-first surfaces carry forward from ADR-018; all URLs are flat (`/jobs`, `/jobs/$jobSlug`, `/jobs/$jobSlug/runs/$runId`, `/targets/$targetSlug`, `/channels/$channelSlug`, `/alert-rules/$ruleSlug`) with no Workspace segment.** The single Workspace is resolved once, server-side, in the `_authenticated` route context, so the slug never appears in a URL or in the chrome. No Workspace switcher. | Customer-nested URLs (ADR-018); a Workspace switcher plus `/workspaces/$slug/…` nesting (built and removed in this pass: a switcher selecting among one Workspace is ceremony); flat ULID-keyed URLs (lose readability). |
| Permissions | **Target CRUD opens to Operators.** Admin retains Workspace lifecycle and global config. The workspace-vs-customer paired server functions collapse to one function per entity. | The ADR-025 / ADR-016 admin gate on Target and on the (now-removed) global scope. |
| Autotask association | **Optional metadata on the Workspace and/or the `autotask` Channel config**, not an org tier. | `customer.autotaskCompanyId` as a structural 1:1 (the MSP framing this ADR retires). |

The alerting evaluator's scope clause simplifies from `(scope='workspace') OR (scope='customer' AND customer_id=?) OR (scope='job' AND job_id=?)` to `(scope='workspace' AND workspace_id=?) OR (scope='job' AND job_id=?)`.

## Consequences

**Positive:**

- The scope lattice drops from three tiers to two. "Where does this Channel live?" has one answer (its Workspace); "which rules fire?" is the union of two sets, not three.
- The on-ramp collapses from "Customer → Target → Job" to "Job," with the Target picked-or-created inline and visible on the Job. The decoupling the user felt between Targets and Jobs is closed without losing Target reuse.
- One time-based concept (`Schedule`) with an NL affordance replaces the cron-vs-interval choice. The two-lane dispatch reality ([ADR-014](014-two-lane-dispatch.md)) becomes an implementation detail the operator never sees.
- The paired `createWorkspace*Fn` / `createCustomer*Fn` server functions for Channels, AlertRules, and Templates collapse to one function per entity, halving that surface.
- The runtime hot path is untouched: cron still rides the Queue lane, interval still rides the per-Job Alarm DO, `dispatchRun()` is the same module. The risk concentrates in migration and IA, not dispatch.

**Negative:**

- This retires the MSP multi-tenant structure [ADR-025](025-workspace-scope-on-channels-and-alert-rules.md) was built to serve. Per-client isolation, per-client alert routing, and per-client reporting no longer have a first-class tier; they return as separate Workspaces (N Workspaces for N isolated clients) or as a future tier behind a new ADR. Teams genuinely running 270 distinct clients with distinct Autotask companies will feel this as a downgrade, not a simplification. The decision is correct only if daily operation is single-Workspace-dominant.
- The term "Workspace" is repurposed: it previously named the global scope above Customer, it now names the former Customer. Every reference (CONTEXT.md, code, ADRs 018/025/026, the in-flight PRD slate) has to be re-read with the new meaning, and the rename touches `customer_id` across `targets`, `jobs`, `runs`, `channels`, `alert_rules`, `job_templates`, `authoring_sessions`, and `customer_secrets`.
- Cross-Workspace shared destinations (one ops Teams channel for every Workspace) are not expressible in v1; they re-materialize as a per-Workspace duplicate or wait for a future shared tier. This is the exact ergonomics ADR-025 optimized, deliberately given back.
- The migration reparents the former global (`scope='workspace'`) Channels and AlertRules into the default Workspace, and former Customer-scoped rows into their Workspace. It is a single-sweep migration but a wide one; for a dev/seed database a rebuild-and-reseed is cleaner than an in-place column dance.

**Neutral / trade-off:**

- `Schedule` is admitted into the ubiquitous language as the name for the time-based Trigger configuration (CONTEXT.md previously listed it under _Avoid_ because it collided with Job). The collision is resolved by scope: `Job` is the entry, `Schedule` is its timing, and the cron string is one serialized form of a Schedule.
- **Single Workspace in v1; multi-workspace deferred.** The tier exists in the schema (every entity keeps `workspace_id`) but the UI commits to one Workspace: no switcher, no `/workspaces/$slug` URLs, the Job form auto-assigns it, and the Jobs/Runs tables, the home lists, and the Run filters drop the Workspace dimension entirely. The one resolution point is `_authenticated.beforeLoad`, which reads the sole Workspace into route context (`context.workspaceSlug`); every child route and server-fn call sources the slug there instead of from a `$workspaceSlug` path param. Re-introducing multiple Workspaces means re-adding that param (or a facet filter) at that single seam plus a management surface, additive, not a re-migration. The cost until then: there is no UI to create, switch, or manage Workspaces, and nothing can be scoped per-client.
- This ADR supersedes the URL-grammar half of [ADR-018](018-navigation-ia.md) (Customer-nested becomes Workspace-context) and carries its Jobs-first navigation principle forward unchanged. It supersedes [ADR-025](025-workspace-scope-on-channels-and-alert-rules.md) in full (the tier it added is removed). It amends the Operator language of [ADR-026](026-cloudflare-access-with-entra-oidc.md) ("across Customers" becomes "across Workspaces") and the Target/global admin gate of [ADR-016](016-operator-authz.md). It reaffirms [ADR-014](014-two-lane-dispatch.md), builds on [ADR-017](017-cron-and-time.md) and [ADR-015](015-ai-authoring-stack.md) for Schedule resolution and NL authoring, and inherits [ADR-023](023-modal-with-pills-crud-convention.md) for the inline pick-or-create flow.
- The in-flight PRD slate (mid Track-A) is reshaped: issues scoped to Customer hubs, per-Customer scope, or the cron/interval split need re-cutting against the consolidated model. That reconciliation is a follow-on, tracked when this ADR is accepted.
